NightShift

Lead Programmer | Unreal Engine 5.7 | In Development

NightShift is a creepy fishing game where the player works the night shift at a remote dock. Each night, the player must catch enough fish to meet a required quota or risk being fired. As the nights progress, increasingly strange events begin to occur around the docks.
